# Product brief — fictional Payments Pilot

Updated: 2026-06-02

The fictional Northstar Cooperative is preparing a small payments pilot for its
internal marketplace. The pilot must support auditable payment records and a
weekly reconciliation report. It is not a production launch. See ADR-014 for
the recorded database decision and HANDOVER-009 for the latest project status.

The product owner is listed as the Payments Product Lead. No personal email,
phone number, or office address is included in the product brief.

## Acceptance criteria

The pilot must reconcile every test payment and retain a source-linked audit
trail. The team has not documented a production launch date or a committed
monthly transaction volume.
